# Effect research and original interpretation

The [manufacturer's P22 product page](https://www.dragonfireworks.com/index.php/consumer-fireworks/p-professional-line/p22-dragons-family-fireworks-package-2-detail) identifies Dragon's Family Fireworks Package 2 and describes colored peony/dahlia blooms, willow and chrysanthemum characteristics, brocade and glitter-like details, plus upright, center-opening and fan arrangements. The [FireworksPH listing](https://fireworksph.com/products/professional-line) corroborates that effect family and uses DFAMILY2. No price, duration, caliber or exact edition claim is used in the landing animation.

Original interpretation: spherical colored trail blooms for peony/dahlia; curved descending gold/silver structures for willow/brocade; upward spread lanes for fan arrangements; small luminous heads for layered sparkling detail. Their layout, colors, geometry and timings are artistic choices. They do not reproduce the real product's exact firing sequence. No physical ignition, package damage or ejection of consumer merchandise is shown. There is no flashing strobe animation or uncontrolled idle motion.

The manufacturer links to a YouTube player (`_DPOUx1UDhY`). Fetches of that player/video failed during research; actual playback was not verified. No third-party footage was downloaded, embedded or claimed as observed. The supplied Spectra screenshot establishes dramatic framing and depth, not the unavailable video's exact camera choreography.

FireStock loaded in the browser: a central dark bust in front of an oversized radial backdrop, large lettering and subsequent editorial blocks. Those central-subject/depth principles inform this original scene. Its assets/source, palette and arbitrary camera movement were not copied or claimed verified.

Research supports the choice of effect families. It does not verify packaging faces, dimensions, certifications, image rights for manufacturer photography or endorsement of Fireworks Inventory.
